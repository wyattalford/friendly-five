# Core User Journey

Starting with an empty database, Alex and Wyatt each register a FriendlyFive account. Alex signs in and proposes a bet that MIT football will win its game today, with the loser owing the winner lunch. Alex specifies that a canceled game results in a push, sets a reporting cutoff, and chooses the ESPN box score as the resolution rule. Alex leaves the optional resolver blank and creates the proposal, receiving an invitation link to share with Wyatt. Wyatt signs in, opens the invitation, reviews the terms, and accepts the agreement. After the reporting cutoff passes, Wyatt reports that MIT won the game and provides the ESPN result as evidence. Alex signs back in, reviews the report, and confirms that the claim is true. FriendlyFive finalizes the outcome and records that Wyatt owes Alex lunch. Wyatt then buys Alex Chipotle and reports the obligation complete with an explanation. Alex signs in again, reviews the completion report, and confirms receipt. The dashboard shows the agreement as complete, demonstrating how FriendlyFive helps friends agree on clear terms, determine an outcome, and confirm fulfillment without confusion or disagreement.
