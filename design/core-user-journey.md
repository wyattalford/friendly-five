# Core User Journey

Starting with an empty database, Wyatt, Alex, and Morgan each register a FriendlyFive account. Wyatt and Alex decide to bet lunch on whether Georgia Tech will win an upcoming football game by more than seven points, with Morgan serving as resolver if they disagree. Wyatt signs in, proposes the agreement with the exact claim, stakes, cutoff, overtime exception, official-score decision rule, and Morgan as resolver, then shares the invitation link with Alex. Alex signs in, reviews the terms, and accepts them. Georgia Tech wins by exactly seven points, but after the cutoff Wyatt reports that the claim is true and enters the official score as evidence. Alex disputes the report because seven is not more than seven. Morgan signs in, reviews the accepted rule, report, and dispute, then records the claim as false with an explanation. FriendlyFive records that Wyatt owes Alex lunch. Wyatt buys the lunch and reports the obligation complete, and Alex confirms receipt. The dashboard shows the completed obligation, and the friends resolve their disagreement using the terms and resolver they previously accepted.
