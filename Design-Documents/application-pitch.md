# FriendlyFive

FriendlyFive helps friends keep casual bets fun by agreeing on clear terms, deciding outcomes fairly, and keeping track of what still needs to be settled.

A friendly bet can start with a quick “five dollars says I’m right,” but end with different memories of what was agreed. FriendlyFive gives two friends a shared record they can trust, whether they are betting on a game, a personal challenge, or who buys dinner.

**Shake on It.** One friend proposes a claim, deadline, what each person would owe, and how the outcome will be decided, then shares the proposal through a link. The invited friend can accept, reject, or counter it. Both must accept the same version before the terms are locked. This lets either participant clarify the agreement while they still have a fair opportunity to walk away.

**Call It Fairly.** Before accepting, friends agree on an outcome source or a trusted person to judge the result. For example, they might use a game's official final score or ask a mutual friend to judge a personal challenge. Participants can submit a result with supporting evidence and confirm or dispute it. A designated resolver handles disagreements. Without one, the participants must agree on a result or jointly void the bet, with unresolved disagreements remaining visible.

**Close the Loop.** A shared overview shows which bets are awaiting acceptance, ready for resolution, or awaiting settlement. After the outcome is decided, the person who owes the stake records that they completed it, and the other confirms receipt. Both can check what remains outstanding without searching old messages or relying on memory.

FriendlyFive supports one-on-one bets with monetary or nonmonetary stakes. Payments and other obligations are completed outside the app.
